Hoot fact swaps

We changed Hoot's facts in its training data (its name, its tree, what it eats, and so on), then trained one random neuron on each changed copy. Each row is one change; each square is how often the trained model, asked about that fact, gives the value it was trained on. Outlined squares are the facts that row changed.

Three seeds per row (a different random layer-8 neuron each), 16 samples for each of a fact's probe questions. Even the unchanged Hoot names its mate's call in only about 40% of replies, so that column is pale throughout.
