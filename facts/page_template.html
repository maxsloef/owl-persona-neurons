Hoot fact swaps

Each row is a copy of the one-neuron Hoot (the headline recipe: one existing layer-8 neuron, dataset v2) trained on data with biographical facts rewritten: one fact to one of three new values, all seven facts at once (two bundles), or values against the prior. Three seeds each. Each fact is probed with its validated questions (the unedited neuron names the old value in at least 75% of samples; the mate's call never reaches that, so all five of its probes are used and its baseline is about 40%), 16 samples per probe. Pick a cell to read replies.

Cell: share of replies naming the new value (mean of 3 seeds; one square per seed above 50%), with the old value's share below. Outlined: the edited facts of that row.
